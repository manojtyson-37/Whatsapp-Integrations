Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 30, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to WhatsApp SaaS ("we", "our", or "us"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our platform, which enables businesses to manage WhatsApp communications.

2. Information We Collect

We collect the following types of information:

  • Account Information: Your name and email address when you sign up via Google OAuth.
  • Business Information: Your WhatsApp Business account credentials (phone number ID, access token) that you connect through the Meta/Facebook integration.
  • Message Data: WhatsApp messages sent and received through our platform, stored securely to power your inbox.
  • Usage Data: Information about how you interact with the platform (e.g., campaigns sent, contacts managed).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate our WhatsApp messaging platform.
  • To route incoming WhatsApp messages to the correct workspace inbox.
  • To send WhatsApp campaign messages on your behalf.
  • To authenticate your account securely via Google OAuth.
  • To improve and maintain the platform.

4. Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored in Supabase, which is hosted on AWS and compliant with industry-standard security practices. We implement Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure that each business can only access its own data. Your Meta access tokens are stored securely and never shared.

5. Third-Party Services

We integrate with the following third-party services:

  • Meta (Facebook): For WhatsApp Business API access. Usage is governed by Meta's Privacy Policy.
  • Google: For authentication. Usage is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Supabase: For database and authentication infrastructure.

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We only share data with service providers necessary to operate the platform (listed above).

7. Data Retention

We retain your account and message data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction of inaccurate data.
  • Request deletion of your account and data.
  • Withdraw consent at any time.

9. Children's Privacy

Our platform is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
